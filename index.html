Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our cyber services, hardware repairs, and retail supplies

1. How long does it take to process an online application?

Most standard portal submissions are completed while you wait (10 to 20 minutes). Complex filings or document uploads are processed promptly on the same day.

2. How do I know what is wrong with my laptop or desktop?

Bring your device to our shop in Makutano for a fast diagnostic check. Our technical team tests the hardware and software to identify the root issue before performing any repairs.

3. How long does a computer repair or software upgrade take?

Basic software installations, OS upgrades, and system cleanups take a few hours. Hardware replacements (such as screens, keyboards, or SSD upgrades) are typically completed within 24 to 48 hours.

4. Are the computer accessories and laptop chargers genuine?

Yes, all our computer peripherals, laptop power adapters, external storage drives, and office stationery items are sourced from verified, trusted manufacturers.
